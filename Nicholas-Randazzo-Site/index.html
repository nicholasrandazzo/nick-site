A developing vocation

Medicine as craft, judgment, and obligation.

Bioethics belongs inside that aspiration, not beside it as an ornament. Questions of consent, risk, rescue, proportionate intervention, professional character, and the meaning of a changed life are part of the work medicine asks us to do.
