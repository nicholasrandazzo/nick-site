Prospective direction

Cardiac surgery as the field ahead.

I am drawn to cardiac surgery by the union it demands: precise knowledge of mechanism, disciplined technical practice, coordinated clinical judgment, and responsibility to patients at moments of exceptional consequence.

My present task is medical formation. I do not claim a current cardiothoracic research appointment; the interests below describe the direction in which I intend to develop during medical school and beyond.
